2. The service

Opaline is a themed calendar app for iOS that beautifully displays and manages the calendars already connected to your iPhone.

  • Switch the grid, event bars, fonts, and widgets across more than 20 themes
  • Display and edit calendars connected through EventKit — such as Apple, iCloud, Google, and Outlook — without any sign-up
  • Home-screen widgets and theme-matched lock-screen wallpapers
  • Month, week, and day views, monthly notes and goals, and quick-add templates
  • Lunar dates and public holidays for users in Korea

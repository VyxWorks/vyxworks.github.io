VyxWorks (“we”) respects the privacy of everyone who plays the iOS game “Munch Munch” (the “App”). This policy explains what information the App handles and how.

Summary. We run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves. Your progress and settings stay on your device. Ads are shown by Google AdMob, and tracking for personalized ads happens only if you allow it. Apple handles payments.

1. Information we collect directly

We do not run servers and we do not collect or store your personal information. Your game progress, candy and booster balances, and settings stay on your device. The daily snack reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device, and it is sent only if you choose to receive it.

2. Information processed by third parties for ads

The App is free and shows ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK from Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud, and manage consent, these SDKs may send the following to Google:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers (the advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking; other app-scoped identifiers)Serving and measuring ads; personalized ads if allowed
Advertising data (ads seen and interactions with them)Serving and measuring ads; analytics
In-app interactions (app launches, ad taps)Improving ad performance; analytics
Approximate location (region estimated from IP address)Serving ads; determining whether consent is required
Performance, diagnostic, and crash dataDiagnosing SDK issues; analytics

Ad requests are limited to content suitable for all ages (G), matching the App's 4+ age rating.

See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. International transfers

The data in section 2 is sent to Google LLC in the United States and other countries where Google operates facilities, when the App loads or shows an ad. Google keeps it for the periods set in its privacy policy. You can contact Google through its privacy contact form.

4. Your choices

  • App Tracking Transparency — you are asked once, after you clear Stage 3. Change it anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Ad privacy choices — where consent is required (the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and similar regions), open the App's Settings → “Ad Privacy Options”.
  • Notifications — turn them off in iOS Settings → Notifications → Munch Munch.
  • Remove Ads — an in-app purchase turns off ads between stages, and the App stops loading them. Optional ads you choose to watch for a reward stay available and load only when you tap one.

Declining tracking does not change the game. Ads may still appear, without personalization.

5. Payments

Apple processes in-app purchases. We never receive card numbers or other payment details. The App stores transaction IDs on your device only, so a purchase is not delivered twice.

6. Retention and deletion

You can erase your game data with Settings → “Erase Progress” or by deleting the App. We keep no copy. If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer, and delete them once they are no longer needed. Data processed by Google follows Google's policies.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (under 14 in Korea), and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or restrict your personal information. We hold none, but you can contact us with any question (section 10). For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy contact form.

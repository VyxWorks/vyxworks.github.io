VyxWorks (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal data Achieve (“the Service”) processes, how we process it, and the rights you can exercise.

Summary. Achieve requires an account, and you sign in with Apple or Google. Your email address and the content you create — to-dos, routines, plans, reflections, and more — are stored on your device and synced through our database, hosted by Supabase in the United States, so they are available on your other devices. Calendar events, notifications, and on-device AI processing stay on your device. There are no ads, analytics, or tracking, and we never sell your data. You can delete your account and all synced data in the app at any time.

1. Personal data we process

We process only the data needed to sign you in and keep your content in sync across your devices.

CategoryItemsSourceWhere it is stored
1.1 Account Email address (with Sign in with Apple, this may be an Apple private relay address), account ID (a random UUID), and the sign-in provider you used (Apple or Google). With Google sign-in, Google may also provide your name and profile-picture URL; the authentication record keeps them, but the app does not display or use them. Sign in with Apple (the app requests only your email address) or Google sign-in Supabase authentication service (United States)
1.2 Content you create To-dos (title, notes, dates and reminders, priority, status, tags, checklist items, and a change log of edits); routines and their daily records and checklists; routine and D-Day folders; D-Days; focus sessions (start and end times, linked item, note); daily plan (BIG 3) items; daily reflections; saved filters; tags; dashboard layout and widget settings, including text you type into widgets Your direct input On your device (local database) and in our database hosted by Supabase (United States)

1.3 Data that stays on your device

The following is processed only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Calendar events — after you grant permission, Achieve reads and writes your device’s calendars through Apple’s EventKit. Events are never uploaded to our servers; they sync only through the calendar accounts you have set up in iOS, iPadOS, or macOS.
  • Notifications — all reminders and notifications are local notifications scheduled on your device. We do not use remote push notifications.
  • App data on the device — app preferences, widget and Live Activity snapshots, and diagnostic logs.
  • On-device AI — tag suggestions (and, in an upcoming update, a suggested first sentence for your daily reflection) are generated by Apple’s on-device language model. The text never leaves your device.

The quote widget downloads a public quote catalog from our server. The catalog contains no personal data.

1.4 Data we do not collect

We do not collect the following:

  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any other data for advertising or tracking
  • Usage analytics or crash reports (Achieve includes no analytics or crash-reporting SDK)

2. Purposes of processing

  1. Account and sign-in — create your account, sign you in with Apple or Google, and identify your account (email address, account ID, sign-in provider).
  2. Storage and sync — store the content you create and keep it in sync so the same data is available on all your devices.

We do not use your personal data for advertising, and we do not sell it.

3. Retention and deletion

3.1 Account and synced data

  • We keep your account information and synced content until you delete your account.
  • When you delete an item in the app, the deletion syncs to your other devices. The item’s last synced content remains in our database until you delete your account.
  • You can delete your account in the app at Settings › Account and sync › Delete account. This immediately deletes your account and all synced data from our database — every table that holds your data is linked to your account and is removed with it. For Sign in with Apple accounts, the app also revokes your Sign in with Apple token.
  • Copies in our hosting provider’s database backups are deleted automatically according to the provider’s backup retention.
  • Uninstalling the app does not delete your account or the data stored on our servers.

3.2 Data on your device

  • Deleting your account also erases the app’s data on the device you delete it from.
  • Signing out uploads any unsynced changes and then erases Achieve’s local data from that device. When you sign in again, your data is downloaded again.

4. Third parties and processors

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not provide it to third parties except where required by law. The following companies process data to operate the Service.

5. Your rights

You can exercise the following rights at any time.

  • Access, correct, delete — view, edit, or delete your content directly in the app.
  • Export — export your to-dos as a CSV file at Settings › Data › Export (CSV).
  • Delete your account — Settings › Account and sync › Delete account (see section 3).
  • Revoke permissions — turn off calendar or notification access in your device’s settings at any time; the related features will stop working.

For other requests — such as access, correction, portability, or objection — contact support@vyxworks.com.

6. Tracking, analytics, and ads

Achieve contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDK. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites and do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), so the app never shows an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt. We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

7. Children’s privacy

Achieve is not directed to children under 14. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 14; if we learn we have, we will delete it promptly. Where local law requires a higher minimum age, that age applies.

8. International transfers

Your account information and content are stored on Supabase’s servers in the United States (AWS region us-east-1, Northern Virginia), so they are transferred to the United States. For users in the EU, EEA, and UK, these transfers are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) in Supabase’s data processing addendum. Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in are processed by Apple and Google on their own global infrastructure.

9. EU/EEA users (GDPR)

  • Legal basis — performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)): we need your account and synced content to provide the Service you signed up for.
  • Rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection.
  • How to exercise them — directly in the app (edit or delete content, export CSV, delete your account) or by email.
  • Transfers — see section 8.
  • Supervisory authority — you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
  • Contact — support@vyxworks.com

10. California residents (CCPA/CPRA)

  • Categories of personal information we collect: identifiers (email address, account ID) and the content you create (see section 1).
  • We do not sell or share personal information.
  • To access, delete, or correct your data, use the app or email support@vyxworks.com.
  • You will not receive discriminatory treatment for exercising these rights.

See section 4 for outsourced processing and section 8 for the overseas transfer.

11.4 Destruction, automatic collection, and safeguards

12. Security

  • Row-level security — every table that holds your data is protected by row-level security, so an account can read and write only its own rows.
  • Encryption in transit — all traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS (HTTPS).
  • Keychain — your sign-in session token is stored in your device’s Keychain.
  • On-device processing — calendar events and the text handled by on-device AI never leave your device.
